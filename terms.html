1. Eligibility and Service Area

Level Health New York serves New York City only. To enroll as a member you must:

  • Be at least 18 years of age;

  • For virtual visits, be physically located in New York State at the time of each consultation.

We do not provide care to individuals who reside outside New York City. If you move outside New York City during your membership, please notify us promptly at support@levelhealth.io so we can discuss continuity of your care.

Level Health New York is a direct-pay, private concierge medical practice. All care is provided personally by Dr. Fajardo-Ahmad, a board-certified Internal Medicine physician licensed in New York State and the practice’s sole physician. Services include, where clinically indicated:
