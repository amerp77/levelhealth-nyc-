Privacy Policy

Effective October 2026  ·  New York City

Level Health New York (“Level Health New York,” “we,” “our,” or “us”), a subsidiary of Level Health, values your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, with whom we share it, and the choices and rights available to you.

This policy applies to this website and any digital services operated by Level Health New York. Protected Health Information (“PHI”) collected as part of clinical care is additionally governed by our Notice of Privacy Practices under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (“HIPAA”).

Service area: Level Health New York provides care exclusively to adults residing in the five boroughs of New York City — Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, The Bronx and Staten Island.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Contact details — first name, last name, email address, phone number, and borough of residence submitted through our consultation request form, along with your confirmation that you reside in New York City.
  • Consultation preferences — your primary interest, membership of interest, preferred day and time, and any notes you choose to include. Please do not include sensitive medical details in the website form.
  • Health intake information — goals, symptoms, history, and any narrative you choose to share when requesting or receiving care.
  • Payment information — billing details processed by our third-party invoicing provider (QuickBooks). We do not store full payment card numbers on our own servers.
  • Clinical information — once you become a member, your physician collects laboratory results, examination notes, prescriptions, and related clinical data. This information is PHI and is governed by our HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device and usage data — IP address, browser type, operating system, pages visited, and timestamps, collected through standard server logs and limited analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Respond to Discovery Call requests and consultation inquiries, and schedule your in-person and virtual appointments.
  • Confirm that you are eligible for care within our New York City service area.
  • Provide medical care, design and adjust your personalized, physician-directed protocol, and coordinate therapeutic fulfillment.
  • Communicate with you about your care, appointments, billing, and changes to our services.
  • Operate, secure, and improve our website and services.
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, and professional obligations, including those under New York law.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to operate Level Health New York and provide your care:

  • Care delivery platforms — Spruce, our HIPAA-compliant platform, supports our virtual visits, secure messaging, scheduling, and documentation. Spruce acts as a Business Associate under HIPAA.
  • Therapeutic fulfillment partners — for therapeutics clinically indicated by your physician, we coordinate fulfillment with partners that deliver directly to you.
  • Diagnostic laboratories — when your physician orders bloodwork, your information is shared with the testing laboratory solely to perform and return your results.
  • Fitness coaching — where your membership includes private fitness coaching sessions, we share only the information needed to personalize your nutrition and training guidance, and only with your consent.
  • Billing and accounting providers — QuickBooks for invoicing and payment processing.
  • Service providers — vendors that help us run our website (hosting, form delivery, analytics) under contractual confidentiality and security obligations.
  • Our parent company — Level Health, for shared administrative, billing, and support functions, subject to the same privacy and HIPAA obligations described here.
  • Legal disclosures — when required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Level Health New York or others.
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice as required by law.

4. Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses a minimal set of cookies and similar technologies necessary to operate the site and, where applicable, limited analytics to understand aggregate site usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings. We do not use cookies to deliver targeted advertising.

5. Data Security

We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information, including encryption in transit, restricted access controls, and Business Associate Agreements with our HIPAA-covered service providers. No transmission or storage system is perfectly secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security but work to maintain industry-standard protections. In the event of a security breach involving your private information, we will notify you as required by HIPAA and the New York SHIELD Act.

6. Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal and recordkeeping obligations (including New York State medical records retention requirements), resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. PHI retention is governed by New York State and federal medical record retention laws.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable New York and federal law, you may:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion, subject to limitations imposed by medical recordkeeping laws.
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at support@levelhealth.io. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling your request. Specific rights regarding PHI are described in our HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our services are intended for adults 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

9. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Effective” date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you. Continued use of our services after updates take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your information, please contact us at support@levelhealth.io.
