/ Your Physician

Dr. Fajardo-Ahmad

Board-Certified Internal Medicine Harlem Hospital Columbia / NewYork-Presbyterian

Dr. Fajardo-Ahmad is a board-certified Internal Medicine physician with over six years of private practice experience in New York. Driven by a deep passion for wellness and preventive care, she is committed to helping her patients achieve their personal health goals, feel their best, and thrive as they age.

She earned her medical degree from Universidad Iberoamericana, then completed her Internal Medicine residency and Nephrology fellowship at Harlem Hospital in New York City, followed by advanced Transplant Nephrology training at Columbia University / NewYork-Presbyterian Hospital — one of the nation’s premier academic medical centers.

It was during that training that she developed a profound appreciation for cellular medicine — the understanding that optimal health begins at the cellular level. Today she brings that integrative, science-driven perspective to every patient she personally treats.

— A New York physician, for New York.
